---
id: faucet-cartridge
title: Fixing a dripping single-handle faucet (US DOE / EPA WaterSense guidance)
url: https://www.epa.gov/watersense/fix-leak-week
---
Turn off the supply valves under the sink and open the faucet to release pressure before taking anything apart. Plug the drain so small parts are not lost.
Remove the handle cap and screw, then the handle, then the retaining nut or clip holding the cartridge. Note the cartridge orientation before pulling it out.
A drip from the spout usually means a worn cartridge or O-rings and seats. Take the old cartridge to a store to match it, or replace O-rings coated with silicone plumber's grease.
Reassemble in reverse order, open the supply valves slowly, and check for leaks at the base and under the sink.
