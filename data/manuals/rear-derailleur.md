title: Rear derailleur adjustment (Park Tool)
url: https://www.parktool.com/en-us/blog/repair-help/rear-derailleur-adjustment
---
Check the derailleur hanger is straight before adjusting anything; a bent hanger causes skipping that cable tension cannot fix.
Set the high limit screw (H) so the upper pulley lines up under the smallest cog. Set the low limit screw (L) so the chain cannot shift past the largest cog into the spokes.
Indexing: shift to the smallest cog, then turn the barrel adjuster. If the chain is slow to climb to larger cogs, add tension by turning the barrel adjuster counterclockwise a quarter turn at a time. If it is slow to drop to smaller cogs, reduce tension.
Set the B-tension screw so the upper pulley sits close to, but not touching, the largest cog.
Noisy or ghost shifting after adjustment usually points to a bent hanger, worn cable or housing, or a worn chain.
