title: Washing machine will not drain: pump filter cleaning (manufacturer manuals, summarized)
url: https://www.whirlpool.com/blog/washers-and-dryers/clean-washer-drain-pump-filter.html
---
Unplug the washer. Place towels and a shallow tray under the access panel at the front bottom of the machine; water will come out.
Open the small drain hose (if present) to empty water slowly, then unscrew the pump filter counterclockwise.
Remove lint, coins, and hair from the filter and the opening. Turn the pump impeller by hand with a finger to check it moves freely.
Refit the filter hand tight, check the drain hose is not kinked, and run a short rinse cycle to confirm it drains. A humming pump that still does not drain may be faulty.
