title: Laptop overheating and fan cleaning (manufacturer service guides, summarized)
url: https://support.hp.com/us-en/document/ish_4102487-4102609-16
---
Shut down, unplug the charger, and if the battery is removable take it out. Hold the button for a few seconds to drain residual power. Work on an anti-static surface.
Blow compressed air into the intake and exhaust vents in short bursts. Hold the fan still with a plastic tool so it does not overspin and damage its bearing.
A fan that grinds, rattles, or never spins needs replacement. Thermal paste older than a few years can be replaced if the service manual allows opening the heatsink.
Do not use a vacuum cleaner on internal parts, because of static discharge.
