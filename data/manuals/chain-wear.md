title: Chain wear and replacement (Park Tool)
url: https://www.parktool.com/en-us/blog/repair-help/when-to-replace-a-chain-on-a-bicycle
---
Chains elongate as pins and rollers wear. Measure with a chain checker: at 0.5% elongation replace the chain on 11 and 12 speed drivetrains; at 0.75% on 10 speed and lower.
A worn chain skips under load, especially on small cogs, and wears the cassette and chainrings. If a new chain skips on the old cassette, the cassette is also worn.
Clean the chain with degreaser, dry it, then apply one drop of lubricant per roller and wipe off the excess. Rust or stiff links should be freed or the chain replaced.
